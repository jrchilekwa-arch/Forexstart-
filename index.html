💡 What is a pip?

A pip is a standard unit used to measure movement in many currency pairs. For most pairs, one pip is 0.0001. For many JPY pairs, one pip is 0.01.

Beginner Tip

Pips measure movement. They do not automatically tell you how much money you made or lost.
